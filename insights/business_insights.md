Key Insights

1. Sales Trend & Forecast
Weekly sales remain stable with sharp seasonal spikes during year-end holidays. Forecasting indicates consistent demand with predictable seasonal variability.

2. Store Performance
A small number of stores contribute disproportionately to total revenue, suggesting opportunities for focused inventory and operational optimization.

3. Holiday Impact
Average weekly sales during holiday weeks are significantly higher than non-holiday weeks, confirming the impact of promotional timing.

4. Seasonality
Sales peak consistently in November–December across years, highlighting predictable year-end demand patterns.
